Story chronology

Timeline

S1–5

Wrong mountain → first kiss

Lucy meets Ryn, learns the truth, defends him without turning him Orthodox, and makes Black Mountain home.

S6–15

World beyond the Register

Taewon, Wei Cang, Vagrant City, the Ancient Houses and Hollow Heaven expand the world while Ryn and Lucy become a real couple. Ryn proposes in S15.

S16–18

People before rankings

The engagement breathes. Lucy meets the Sovereigns, Pillars and Great Lords as people before the Eclipse Court attacks those identities.

S19–20

Eclipse Court and succession

Kassian Ro dies protecting populated villages. Sael becomes #5, Tessan enters at #20, and Ryn/Lucy deliberately leave the wedding date blank.

S21–24

The Silent Age

Mu-gyeol and five named Severed Stars force the world to confront martial dependence. Lucy answers with reform while Ryn holds the qi decline long enough for a collaborative solution.

S25

When Ready

Six months later, Ryn and Lucy choose their own date. Family, grief, Kassian’s empty chair, vows, comedy and community receive a full season before the wedding.
