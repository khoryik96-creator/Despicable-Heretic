Romance & story chronology

Timeline

Seasons 1–5

From wrong mountain to first kiss

Lucy meets the infamous hermit, chooses him over his reputation, and ends the first saga calling Black Mountain home.

Season 6

They become a real couple

Awkward breakfast, accidental “boyfriend” classification, deliberate hand-holding, and Rank #1 Seo Taewon entering their lives.

Season 7

Trust without ownership

Lucy dismantles Wei Cang’s conspiracy while Ryn learns to follow her lead even when someone uses her as leverage.

Season 8

Lucy chooses publicly

A political marriage crisis tests them without cheap jealousy. Lucy refuses to become treaty currency and publicly takes Ryn’s hand.

Seasons 9–10

Home grows around them

Vagrant City expands the world; refugees turn Black Mountain into a community; Ryn and Lucy finally say “I love you.”

Seasons 11–13

Partners in war

Lucy builds the coalition and becomes its strategist. Ryn learns he cannot be everywhere and begins trusting the people around him.

Season 14

Love is not control

Mo Yejun tries to frame Lucy as the person who should make Ryn rule. She rejects the idea that loving someone gives ownership of their choices.

Season 15

Make it official

Lucy breaks Hollow Heaven’s command network, Ryn defeats the Empty Throne without claiming it, and then proposes while repairing the porch.

World bible

Lore

The Black Register

Restricted Pavilion archive for people normal ranking cannot describe. Ryn is Entry Three; Mo Yejun was Entry Two; Entry One remains a future mystery.

Hollow Heaven

An old martial compact predating the modern Pavilion. Its philosophy fractured around whether exceptional strength should create political sovereignty.

Vagrant City

A deliberately unranked neutral city filled with masters who refuse public classification and sect authority.

Black Mountain

Begins as Ryn’s hermitage, becomes Lucy’s home, then a free township and emotional center of the coalition without becoming Ryn’s kingdom.

Romance rule

Protection never becomes ownership. Lucy keeps political and martial agency; Ryn’s growth is learning to choose connection, trust, and shared responsibility.

Power rule

Ryn remains absurdly beyond comparison, but lower ranks retain narrative weight through feats, specialized roles, strategy, sacrifice, and victories Ryn does not steal.
