Romance & story chronology

Timeline

Season 1

Wrong mountain, right stranger

Lucy meets Ryn, annoys him into sheltering her, and discovers the hermit is the infamous Despicable Heretic.

Season 2

Trust becomes habit

Lucy learns Ryn’s past, begins training with him, and realizes his practical excuses routinely hide acts of kindness.

Season 3

He chooses to come with her

Ryn enters Lucy’s political world. Private hand-holding, a disastrous dance, and quiet dependence push them beyond simple friendship.

Season 4

The world notices

They stop pretending every touch is accidental. Ryn keeps Lucy’s chipped gift; Lucy sees the Black Register and chooses the man over the myth.

Season 5

Home

Lucy defends Ryn without sanitizing who he is. He builds her a room at Black Mountain, and their slow-burn finally becomes a mutual kiss.

World bible

Lore

The Black Register

A sealed Pavilion archive for entities ordinary ranking cannot meaningfully categorize. Ryn is its only active entry revealed so far.

Why Ryn Is a Heretic

He is not secretly Orthodox. He rejects institutional martial law, judges actions by his own morality, and refuses to validate the Five Sects merely because they possess authority.

Black Mountain

A forbidden mountain that becomes the emotional center of the series: first Ryn’s refuge, then the place Lucy chooses to call home.

Romance rule

Ryn and Lucy’s relationship grows through trust, mundane intimacy, humor, protection without ownership, and Lucy retaining full political and martial agency.
