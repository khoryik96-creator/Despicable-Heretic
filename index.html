Story chronology

Timeline

S1–15

Mountain → Hollow Heaven

Lucy finds Ryn, makes Black Mountain home, becomes his partner, and eventually accepts his proposal after the Register and old martial order fail to contain what he represents.

S16–25

Eclipse → Silent Age → Marriage

The Top 20 become people rather than rank cards, Kassian dies, the Register changes, Mu-gyeol attacks cultivation itself, and Ryn/Lucy marry only when they are ready.

S26–35

Continents → Guild War → Open Gate

Foreign continents arrive, the Pavilion betrayal enables Iron Concord invasion, Azure Dragon and Jade Moon fall as institutions, Orun and Toren die, and Ryn/Lucy found Open Gate.

S36–41

Open Gate grows → Seven Thrones

Open Gate receives a true hierarchy, Varkesh and the Seven Thrones enter the story, the World Meridian is discovered, and the Restoration Mandate tests whether the new institution survives betrayal without becoming the old sects.

S42–45

Maren → Empty Rank → World Meridian

Maren #4 dies saving millions, Sorin enters the Top 20 after a visible vacancy, Han dies as Open Gate’s first disciple memorial, and Lucy’s Meridian Compact ends Kaor’s forced equalization without giving ownership to Ryn or Open Gate.
