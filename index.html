Story chronology

Timeline

S1–15

Mountain → Hollow Heaven

Lucy finds Ryn, Black Mountain becomes home, the Register proves incomplete, and Ryn refuses the Empty Throne before proposing.

S16–25

Eclipse → Silent Age → Marriage

The Top 20 become people before targets, Kassian dies, Mu-gyeol attacks cultivation itself, and Ryn/Lucy marry only when ready.

S26–35

Continents → Guild War → Open Gate

Iron Concord invades, Azure Dragon and Jade Moon fall as old institutions, Orun and Toren die, Open Gate is founded, and No Heaven Above Me is revealed.

S36–45

Seven Thrones → World Meridian

Open Gate gains a hierarchy, Maren dies saving millions, Sorin enters the Top 20, and Lucy creates the Meridian Compact.

S46–50

Sakura Spring → Broken Names → Thousand Lanterns

Spring of the Open Gate awakens, Teren/Meira/Oris join the mountain, Dalen and Rook retire alive, Kesh enters the Top 20, and Open Gate’s global information network comes online.

S51–55

Rank Hunter → Taewon’s Last Sky → Lucy #5

Kaiven climbs through the upper Register and kills Taewon #1. Veyra becomes #1, Lucy becomes #5, Meira returns at #8, and Ryn reveals The World Beneath Falling Blossoms plus One Blossom Buries Heaven.
