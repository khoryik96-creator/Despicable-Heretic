Story chronology

Timeline

S1–15

Mountain, Register, Hollow Heaven

Lucy finds Ryn, makes Black Mountain home, becomes his partner, and eventually accepts his proposal after wars that reveal the Register is not the entire world.

S16–25

Eclipse, Silent Age, Marriage

The ranked cast becomes personal, Kassian dies, the Register changes, Mu-gyeol attacks cultivation itself, and Ryn/Lucy finally marry in Season 25.

S26–30

The sea opens

Arclune arrives, the Pavilion betrayal is exposed, Azure Dragon Hall falls, Orun dies, and Jade Moon dissolves from internal collaboration and civil fracture.

S31–35

Open Gate and the Guild War

Ryn and Lucy reluctantly found Open Gate, train a first generation of elites, face the Iron Concord’s continental army, lose Toren, and force an intercontinental settlement.
