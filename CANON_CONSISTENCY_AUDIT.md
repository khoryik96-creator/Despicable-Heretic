# The Despicable Heretic — Canon Consistency Audit

Last major audit: 2026-08-25

This file is the continuity guardrail for future writing. The manuscript prose is primary canon; metadata, wiki pages, rankings, and the static reader must follow it rather than inventing alternate versions.

## Core invariants

- Ryn is **The Despicable Heretic**, Black Register Entry III, and **UNRANKABLE**.
- Princess Lucy remains a co-protagonist with political, investigative, strategic, martial, emotional, and romantic agency.
- Black Mountain is a home and free township, not Ryn’s kingdom.
- The Heavenly Register remains prestigious. Ryn exceeding it does not make ranked fighters fodder.
- Seo Taewon remains alive and **Rank #1** throughout all 25 published seasons.
- A difficult matchup or one defeat does not automatically overwrite overall rank.
- Important fighters should be introduced as people before the story depends on them as matchup pieces.
- Elements are qi/cultivation expressions, not Western magic.
- Ryn’s named arts remain rare; **Still Sky** stays exceptional.

## Relationship timeline

- S1–S4: trust, attraction, teasing, increasing emotional reliance.
- S5: Black Mountain becomes Lucy’s explicit home; first mutual kiss.
- S6–S10: Ryn and Lucy become an actual couple and say they love each other.
- S11–S15: relationship continues through Hollow Heaven; Ryn proposes at the end of S15.
- S16–S19: they live through engagement, family, wedding planning, ranked-character introductions, and the Eclipse war.
- S20: **no wedding**. After Kassian’s death and the Eclipse Court climax, Lucy and Ryn deliberately leave the wedding date blank and write **WHEN READY**.
- S21–S24: engagement continues through the Silent Age and Quiet Gate crisis.
- S25: six months after the Quiet Gate closes, they choose an autumn date. The entire season handles preparations, family, grief, vows, ceremony, reception, and their first married morning.
- Current end-state: **Ryn and Lucy are married in Season 25**.

## Rank Five continuity

### Early appearance
The unnamed **Rank Five / Fifth Sovereign** who appears during the Season 5 Supreme Dao trial is canonically **Kassian Ro**.

The Season 5 scene presents him almost entirely through overwhelming rank and presence because Lucy does not yet know him personally. Season 16 is therefore a **personal reintroduction**, not the first time Lucy has ever physically seen him. S16 reveals the loud, warm, kitchen-obsessed man behind the intimidating earlier Sovereign encounter.

Do not create a second unnamed former #5 to explain the S5 wording.

### Kassian’s death
- Kassian is Rank #5, Ash-Furnace Sovereign, through Season 19.
- He defeats the Ash King after a prolonged fight that catastrophically damages his lungs.
- During S19 Ch10, Aurel’s Eclipse anchors turn populated heat infrastructure into a catastrophe.
- Kassian opens his already-damaged Furnace Body and makes himself the destination of the runaway heat.
- The villages survive.
- His core and meridians are consumed beyond healing.
- His final concern is whether the villages are safe; his final joke is about seasoning eggs.
- **Kassian dies in Season 19.**

## Reader rank-badge rules

- Full names and ordinary shortened-name references receive rank badges throughout the novel.
- Badges use the rank held **at that chapter**, not the final current roster retroactively.
- S1–S19 use the pre-succession ranks.
- S20 Ch1 and the early portions of Ch2 remain transitional.
- Explicit prose such as `Rank #6 Sael Vardon` or the formal `#5 — Sael Vardon` assignment takes precedence over automatic history inside the transition chapter.
- From S20 Ch3 onward the new roster applies.
- Posthumous Kassian mentions after S19 display **#5 †**, not an active current #5 badge.
- Tessan displays #21 when named before the succession and #20 afterward.
- Seo Taewon remains #1 everywhere.
- Bookmark and Continue Reading localStorage keys must not be changed during reader refactors.

## Ranked-character introduction standard

Avoid: `Name → rank → element → feat → next name`.

Before a major battle depends on an important named fighter, establish at least one of the following, preferably several:
- civilian role or responsibility;
- personality or humor;
- moral belief;
- relationship to another character;
- flaw or failed matchup;
- noncombat habit;
- reason their fighting style developed;
- consequence they care about beyond winning.

S16–S18 second-edition chapters are the reference standard for Top-20 introductions.

Examples:
- Veyra is a physician before poison becomes the point.
- Sael is a drylands administrator before terrain domination becomes the point.
- Nyra is shown protecting a children’s clinic before her thread field becomes the point.
- Boran arrives carrying bridge material before his body-cultivation feat.
- Seline treats patients and orders Ryn to boil water before her combat-healing distinction matters.
- Tessan is present as a quiet #21 analyst before promotion.

## Silent Age timeline

- Yeon Mu-gyeol is Black Register Entry I, **The First Silence**.
- He originally pursued law, treaties, access reform, and nonlethal restraint before concluding reform would always fail.
- His five Severed Stars are:
  - Neris Kaun — Star of Still Blood
  - Rath Odan — Star of Quiet Storms
  - Caldrin Mott — Star of Dry Seas
  - Hessa Dorn — Star of Broken Ground
  - Varo Kest — Star of Quiet Breath
- Each has a personal grievance rooted in martial abuse.
- S22 cross-training teaches ranked fighters not to equate identity with signature art.
- S23 makes ambient-qi loss an infrastructure/public crisis rather than only a combat gimmick.
- Mu-gyeol defeats Taewon under severance-dominated conditions in S23, but **does not take Rank #1**.
- Lucy’s Counter-Register does not simply defend the old martial system. It introduces backup infrastructure, civilian access, oversight, liability, and ways to challenge abuse without removing everyone’s ability to cultivate.
- The Severed Stars begin making independent choices.
- Mu-gyeol helps dismantle the Quiet Gate in S24. His plan is defeated; he is not killed or brainwashed into instant friendship.
