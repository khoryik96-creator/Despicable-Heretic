## Arc identity

**The War Beyond the Register** opens the world beyond the original continent and permanently changes the martial order.

The saga establishes:
- Arclune and its Guild State system;
- the Iron Concord Guild as Arclune’s No.1 military guild;
- Sahraim and the Nacre Isles as distinct foreign civilizations rather than background labels;
- the Pavilion Master’s old contingency pact as the true institutional betrayal behind the invasion;
- Corven Ash’s apparent betrayal as Lucy’s counterintelligence operation;
- the fall of Azure Dragon Hall;
- the dissolution of Jade Moon Sect through collaboration and civil fracture;
- Orun Bale’s death in Season 29;
- Toren Vahl’s death in Season 33;
- seeded successors Dalen Korr and Iseul Venn rather than unexplained replacement characters;
- Ryn and Lucy founding Open Gate because emergency coalitions cannot remain dependent on famous elites arriving in time;
- Ryn becoming **Mountain Teacher** and Lucy **First Steward**;
- the Iron Concord Guild War ending through strategic collapse, defections, negotiated withdrawal, and Ryn’s final individual victory over Cyran Vol;
- **No Heaven Above Me ✧ Supreme** becoming the single revealed Supreme art in Season 34;
- **Unwritten Law ✧ Supreme** remaining sealed;
- **The Last Quiet Beneath Heaven ★ Ultimate** remaining hard locked.

## Canon outcomes by season

### Season 26
Foreign contact expands the known martial world. Arclune, Sahraim, and Nacre are introduced as distinct systems. The Iron Concord’s interest in the eastern continent is already too informed to be innocent.

### Season 27
The Iron Concord and its internal culture are established before full hostility. Corven Ash appears to betray the Register as part of Lucy’s counterintelligence operation. Ryn reveals additional lower-tier formal skill material without touching another Supreme.

### Season 28
The real leak is traced to the Pavilion Master’s old foreign contingency pact. The Celestial Record Pavilion loses moral authority and is forced toward transparency and distributed oversight. Dalen Korr and Iseul Venn are seeded before later succession.

### Season 29
The invasion becomes full-scale war. Azure Dragon Hall falls as a physical institution. **Orun Bale dies** preserving evacuation routes. Ryn’s healing skill is formally revealed while preserving the absolute no-resurrection rule.

### Season 30
The Register mourns and moves. Dalen Korr enters the Top 20 only after prior introduction. Jade Moon’s collaboration crisis escalates.

### Season 31
Jade Moon Sect dissolves rather than being conveniently restored. The old phrase “Five Great Orthodox Sects” stops being literally true. Ryn and Lucy confront the need for a permanent institution capable of producing adaptable people who can act without Ryn.

### Season 32
Ryn and Lucy found **Open Gate**. Ryn insists it is a school; everyone else calls it a sect. The first cohort develops through training, failure, logistics, rescue, and independent deployments rather than instantly becoming world-class monsters.

### Season 33
The Guild War spreads across multiple fronts. **Toren Vahl dies** containing a cascading Concord relay failure after civilians and soldiers escape. **Ten Thousand Roads Return ✦ Transcended** receives a major reveal/application. Iseul’s later promotion is earned through prior relevance.

### Season 34
The Grand Concord produces the saga’s highest military escalation. Ryn reveals **No Heaven Above Me ✧ Supreme** as the one permitted Supreme reveal. It remains qualitatively different from ordinary technique escalation. No second Supreme and no Ultimate appear.

### Season 35
Lucy dismantles the invasion as a system rather than pretending one duel ends an intercontinental war. Withdrawal corridors, medical guarantees, defections, foreign politics, and postwar consequences all matter. Ryn defeats Cyran Vol only after soldiers are no longer being used as the Guildmaster’s strategic shield. Open Gate survives as a permanent institution.

## Permanent quality rules carried forward

The mistakes from the original compressed S16–25 draft must not return:
- Fast pace means forward momentum, not short summary chapters.
- Important foreign fighters are introduced as people before boss fights.
- Ranked fighters remain genuinely monstrous even when Ryn exceeds them.
- Deaths require setup and aftermath.
- Successors are seeded before promotion whenever possible.
- Lucy decides major strategic outcomes every season.
- Ryn does not solve every battlefield personally.
- Marriage remains present through ordinary affection, disagreements, jokes, and shared decisions.
- Ryn’s skill development should prefer deeper applications and tier evolution over endless new move names.
